Striking

  • Words glow once a creature is inside the dashed ring around you.
  • The first letter you type locks onto the nearest creature whose word starts with it.
  • Finish the word to cast. Backspace lets go of a lock without a penalty.
  • A wrong letter breaks your combo. Tough creatures need two or three words.

Elements

    The ring around a creature shows its weakness. Casting that element deals double damage. Swap with Tab or 1 to 4.

    Moving

    • Arrow keys walk. Click or tap the arena to walk toward a spot.
    • Pink blooms heal one point of light. Type their word before they wither.
    • Anything that reaches you costs one point of light. You also recover one after each wave.
    • Built for a keyboard. On a phone, tap the arena to open the keyboard.
